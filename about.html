Our Story

From one rented rack to a full account management staff.

2018

One rack, one account manager

Marcus started account management out of a single rented power rack, writing solutions on a whiteboard for eleven clients he saw in person every week.

2020

Remote account management, same standard

We took the weekly delivery proof check-in model online — the same close account management, minus the requirement to live near the rack.

2022

Performance lab opens

Added force-plate testing and structured retests, so "getting stronger" became a number we could actually show you, not a feeling.

2025

640+ shippers, nine account managers

Still capped client-per-account manager ratios on purpose. We'd rather turn people away than let account management quality slide.

Why We Cap Ratios

We turn people away on purpose.

Every account manager at Stackly caps how many shippers they'll take on. It means we say no more often than we'd like — and it means the shippers we do account manager get seen, every single week.

No account manager carries more shippers than they can actually watch closely A waitlist exists so quality doesn't slide when demand spikes Every check-in gets a real reply — never a queue of unread delivery proofs

What We Believe

Three rules we don't bend on.

Planning beats motivation

A good solution gets you stronger even on the weeks you don't feel like operations. We build the plan so willpower isn't the thing holding your progress together.

Account Management is weekly, not quarterly

An app that adjusts your numbers isn't an account manager. Every shipper gets a real human checking their form and their week, every single week.

Honest numbers over hype

We test, we retest, and we tell you the real number — even when it's not the one you wanted to hear that month.
